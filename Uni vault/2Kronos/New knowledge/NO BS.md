


**1. Most people are following the wrong path into tech in 2025:**

- Building generic projects (like weather apps) and staying stuck in "tutorial hell."
    
- Learning skills that AI is already replacing.
    
- Competing for fewer entry-level jobs without standing out.
    

**2. The real key to breaking into tech:**

- It's not about just learning to code — it’s about **becoming irreplaceable** in an AI-driven world.
    
- Companies hire **problem solvers**, not people who can just follow tutorials.
    

**3. The no-BS roadmap for success:**

- **Step 1:** Stop building generic projects — solve a real problem you care about.
    
- **Step 2:** Start building immediately — learn what you need as you go (embrace messy, uncomfortable learning).
    
- **Step 3:** Focus on developing AI-proof skills:
    
    - Architectural thinking
        
    - Debugging mastery
        
    - Communication skills
        
    - Understanding business context
        
- **Step 4:** Position yourself as a **specialist**, not a generalist.
    
- **Step 5:** Start building your reputation now — share your journey publicly (LinkedIn, GitHub, blogs).
    

**4. Why waiting and doing it alone doesn’t work:**

- Hesitating or trying to "figure it out alone" wastes time and opportunities.
    
- Structured mentorship and accountability are crucial to success.
    

**5. Final takeaway:**

- Success comes from **taking action now**, not overthinking.
    
- Every day you wait, the tech landscape gets more competitive and AI advances further.
