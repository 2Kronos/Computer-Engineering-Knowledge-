
The City of Cape Town's Public Works Department is undertaking a project to modernize its infrastructure maintenance tracking. The current system relies on paper forms and disparate spreadsheets, leading to delays in addressing issues like burst water pipes, potholes, and faulty streetlights. The goal is to develop a centralized, dynamic system that not only tracks maintenance requests but also visualizes the entire network and the relationships between assets. Some of the tools under consideration for this task include:

·      MongoDB

·      MySQL Workbench

·      Lucidchart / Draw.io

·      Neo4j

·      phpMyAdmin

·      Microsoft Visio

1.    Choose any two tools to describe and explain the principles. Provide relevant screenshots and explanations to demonstrate your thorough understanding of the tools.                                                            **20 marks.**

2.    Compare and contrast the features of any two tools. Why would one tool be chosen over the other? Use appropriate examples, screenshots and explanations.                                                                          **20 marks**.

3.    Choose any tool and create a database using the tool. The database must address the given scenario.                                           **30 marks.**

4.    Evaluate all the given tools, in the context of the given scenario. The limitations and underlying assumptions of each tool must be clear.       **30 marks.**

5.    You are expected to have a minimum of 15 references. (Negative marking applies)
