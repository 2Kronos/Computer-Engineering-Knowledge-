
### 📌 Attributes on Relationships

- Attributes **can be added to relationships**, not just to entities.
    
- This is useful when **information depends on the connection between two entities**.
    

#### 🧾 Example: `Advertises` Relationship

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/92ee79d0-6a86-4d47-8039-5134f0fda08a)


- Connects: `Newspaper` 📰 and `PropertyForRent` 🏠
    
- Attributes:
    
    - `dateAdvert` (the date the property was advertised)
        
    - `cost` (the cost of the advertisement)
        
- These details **don't belong to** just the newspaper or the property—they are specific to the **act of advertising**.
    

---

### 🧩 How It's Drawn (ERD)

- Attributes on relationships are **drawn like entity attributes**, but:
    
    - **Connected with dashed lines** to the relationship.
        
    - This shows they **belong to the relationship**, not to either entity directly.
        

---

### 💡 Design Insight

- If a relationship has **many attributes**, it might actually represent a **hidden entity**.
    
    - In our example, `Advertises` might really be an `Advert` entity (something concrete with its own properties like date and cost).
