## 10.1 Information Systems and Databases

### 📘 Definition: Information System

> The **resources** (people, processes, hardware, software, data) that enable the **collection**, **management**, **control**, and **dissemination** of information across an organization.

---

### 🧩 Databases in IS Infrastructure

- Since the 1970s, **database systems** have largely replaced **file-based systems**.
    
- Recognition of **data** as a key corporate resource led to dedicated teams:
    
    - **Data Administration (DA):** Oversees corporate data policies and standards.
        
    - **Database Administration (DBA):** Manages the physical database environment (installation, tuning, backup).
        

---

### 🛠️ Components of a Computer-Based Information System

1. **Database** (stores the data)
    
2. **Database Software** (DBMS)
    
3. **Application Software** (interfaces for users)
    
4. **Hardware** (servers, storage, network)
    
5. **Personnel** (users, developers, DBAs, administrators)
    

> The **database** is central—its design and operation must align with organizational needs.
