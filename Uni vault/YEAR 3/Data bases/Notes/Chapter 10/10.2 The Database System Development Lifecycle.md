
#### **🔹 Database System in the Broader IS**  
- A **database system** is a **core part** of an organization’s **Information System (IS)**.  
- Its **development lifecycle is interconnected** with the **IS lifecycle** (from Section 10.1).  



#### **🔄 Feedback Loops (Not Strictly Linear!)**  
- The process is **iterative**—issues in later stages (e.g., **design flaws**) may require revisiting earlier steps (e.g., **requirement analysis**).  
- **Figure 10.1** shows key feedback loops (but not all possible ones).  

#### **📈 Scalability Matters!**  
- **Small databases** (few users, simple queries) → **Simpler lifecycle**.  
- **Medium/Large databases** (100s-1000s of users, complex apps) → **Highly complex lifecycle**.  
- This chapter **focuses on medium/large systems** (most real-world cases).  

#### **📖 Key Takeaway**  
Database development is **not a straight line**—it’s a **flexible, iterative process** that must adapt to changing needs.
