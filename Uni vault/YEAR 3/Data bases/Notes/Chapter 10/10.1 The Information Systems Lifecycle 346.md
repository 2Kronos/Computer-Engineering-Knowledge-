

#### **🔹 What is an Information System (IS)?**  
An **Information System (IS)** consists of all the resources (people, tech, processes) that enable an organization to **collect, manage, control, and distribute information**.  

#### **📜 Evolution of Database Systems**  
- Since the **1970s**, databases replaced **file-based systems** as the backbone of corporate data management.  
- Organizations now treat **data as a vital resource**, leading to dedicated teams:  
  - **📂 Data Administration (DA)** – Manages **data policies & governance**.  
  - **💾 Database Administration (DBA)** – Oversees **database implementation & maintenance**.  

#### **⚙️ Components of an IS**  
A **computer-based IS** includes:  
✔ **Database** (Stores structured data)  
✔ **Database Software** (e.g., DBMS like MySQL, Oracle)  
✔ **Application Software** (Programs using the database)  
✔ **Hardware** (Servers, storage, networking)  
✔ **Personnel** (Users, developers, admins)  

#### **🔄 The IS Lifecycle Stages**  
Developing an IS (and its database) follows these phases:  
1. **📝 Planning** – Define goals & strategy.  
2. **🔍 Requirements Collection & Analysis** – Understand user & business needs.  
3. **🎨 Design** – Create the database & system structure.  
4. **🧪 Prototyping** – Build a test version for feedback.  
5. **🚀 Implementation** – Develop & deploy the system.  
6. **🧩 Testing** – Ensure everything works correctly.  
7. **🔄 Conversion** – Migrate from old to new systems.  
8. **🔧 Operational Maintenance** – Monitor, update, and improve.  

#### **🔎 Key Insight**  
- A **database is not standalone**—it must align with the **broader IS strategy** of the organization.  
- Terms like **“functional area”** (e.g., Marketing, HR) refer to specific business units that use the IS.  

### **🎯 Why This Matters**  
Understanding the **IS lifecycle** ensures databases are designed **efficiently, sustainably, and in sync with business needs**—not just as tech projects but as **strategic assets**.
