
### **🔍 Summary of Section 10.9: Prototyping**  

#### **🔹 What is Prototyping?**  
Building a **working model** of the database system to:  
- **Test functionality** & **gather user feedback**.  
- **Clarify requirements** before full implementation.  

#### **🎯 Purpose of Prototyping**  
- Identify **what works well** and what’s **inadequate**.  
- Allow users to **suggest improvements** or new features.  
- **Reduce risk** by validating design early.  

---

### **🛠️ Two Prototyping Strategies**  

| **Strategy**               | **Description**                                                                 | **When to Use**                          |  
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|  
| **Requirements Prototyping** | Prototype is **discarded** after clarifying requirements.                       | Early stages, when requirements are unclear. |  
| **Evolutionary Prototyping** | Prototype **evolves** into the final system through iterations.                 | Agile projects, where flexibility is key. |  

#### **Key Differences**  
- **Requirements Prototyping**:  
  - Cheap + quick.  
  - Focuses **only on gathering feedback** (throwaway).  
- **Evolutionary Prototyping**:  
  - Gradually refined into the final product.  
  - Saves time long-term but requires careful planning.  

---

### **💡 Why Prototype?**  
1. **User Engagement**: Users interact with a tangible model, reducing misunderstandings.  
2. **Cost-Effective**: Fix design flaws **early** (cheaper than post-implementation changes).  
3. **Feasibility Testing**: Validate technical challenges (e.g., performance, UI flow).  

**Example**:  
- A **property rental prototype** might mock up:  
  - Search functionality.  
  - Lease agreement forms.  
  - User dashboards.  
- Users test it and request changes (e.g., "Add a photo upload feature").  

---

### **⚠️ Challenges**  
- **Scope Creep**: Users may demand endless tweaks.  
- **Over-Reliance**: Prototypes may lack scalability/security of final systems.  

**Best Practice**:  
- Set **clear goals** for the prototype (e.g., "Test search speed, not payment processing").  
- Use **tools** like Figma (UI) or SQL sandboxes (database logic).  

---

### **🚀 Key Takeaway**  
Prototyping bridges the gap between **design** and **user needs**, ensuring the final system meets real-world demands.  

**Next Step**: After prototyping, move to **implementation** (Section 10.10).
