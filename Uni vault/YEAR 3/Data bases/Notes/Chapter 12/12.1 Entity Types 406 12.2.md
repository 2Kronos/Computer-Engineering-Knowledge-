# 12.1 Entity Types

**What's an Entity Type?**
- A category or group of things that all share the same characteristics.
- These things are important enough to the business that we need to track them separately.
- Can be physical things you can touch (like Staff, Property) or abstract concepts (like a Viewing, a Sale).

**Simple Explanation:**
- Think of an entity type as a "noun" - a person, place, thing, or concept that matters to the business.

**Entity Occurrence (The Actual Thing)**
- A single, specific instance of an entity type.
- Example: If "Staff" is the entity type, then "John Smith (employee ID: SG37)" is one entity occurrence.

**How We Draw It:**
- We use a rectangle with the name inside.
- Names are singular and capitalized (like "Staff" not "staffs").

**Real Examples from DreamHome:**
- Staff (the employees)
- Branch (the office locations)
- PropertyForRent (houses/apartments for rent)
- PrivateOwner (people who own properties)
