# Objectives of the Investigation

## Explain in detail how you plan to solve the engineering or technical problem.

The engineering problem involves designing a controller for a solar-powered washing machine that can adapt to variations in available electrical power. To address this, the investigation will follow a structured approach:

1. **Research and Analysis**: Conduct a thorough review of existing literature on solar power systems, washing machine technology, and Arduino-based control systems. This will provide a foundation for understanding the technical requirements and constraints.

2. **Component Identification**: Analyze the basic components of a direct drive washing machine, such as the motor, water inlet valve, drain pump, and heating element. Determine how these components can be emulated or controlled using an Arduino and commonly available devices.

3. **Energy Assessment**: Evaluate the power consumption of different washing machine cycles (wash, rinse, spin) to understand the energy demands. Special attention will be given to the impact of water heating, as it is a significant energy consumer.

4. **Solar Power Integration**: Investigate the factors affecting solar panel output, such as sunlight availability, panel efficiency, and inverter performance. This will help in sizing the solar power system to meet the washing machine's energy needs.

5. **Control System Design**: Develop a conceptual design for the Arduino-based control system, including algorithms to manage washing cycles and adapt to fluctuating solar power availability. The design will prioritize cold water washing to minimize energy consumption.

6. **Comparative Analysis**: Compare different power source combinations (e.g., solar panels with/without batteries, direct DC operation) and washing machine connection options to identify the most feasible solution.

7. **Simulation and Testing**: Use simulation tools like Tinkercad to test the control system's functionality under various solar energy conditions. This will validate the design before physical implementation.

8. **Budget and Planning**: Compile a detailed budget for all required components and create a project timeline to ensure systematic progress.

9. **Documentation**: Document all findings, designs, and methodologies in a comprehensive report to serve as a reference for future development phases.

## Describe in bullet point form how you plan to solve the problem that you identified.

- **Research**: Study solar power systems, washing machine components, and Arduino control mechanisms.
- **Analyze Power Consumption**: Measure energy usage for each washing cycle, focusing on cold water washing to reduce demand.
- **Solar System Sizing**: Determine the appropriate solar panel and inverter specifications based on energy needs.
- **Design Control Logic**: Develop Arduino algorithms to manage washing cycles and adapt to solar power fluctuations.
- **Evaluate Connection Options**: Compare different power source and washing machine configurations (e.g., AC vs. DC, with/without batteries).
- **Simulate System**: Test the control system in a virtual environment to ensure functionality.
- **Plan Prototype**: Outline steps for building a physical prototype, including component procurement and assembly.
- **Budgeting**: Estimate costs for all hardware and software components.
- **Document Findings**: Compile results, designs, and recommendations in a detailed report.

This approach ensures a systematic investigation into the feasibility of a solar-powered, Arduino-controlled washing machine, addressing both technical and practical challenges.
